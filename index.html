The feedback loop

Using an assistant is not one-and-done. You are driving: ask, wait, read what you got, then choose what happens next. That round trip is the feedback loop.

  1. Prompt — You say what you want (and what you will not accept).
  2. Analyze — Take in what is there and figure out what matters.
  3. Make Changes — Revise, refine, or redirect based on what you found.
  4. Evaluate — Judge whether it is ready, needs another pass, or should change course.

In the story below, quality is your scoreboard: clear briefs, checks, and patient iteration push it up; vague asks and skipped reads drag it down. No report card — just a live read on where your habits land.
